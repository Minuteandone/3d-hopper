What is this?

This is a work-in-progress reverse-engineered web port of the E3 2010 Tech Demo: 3D Hopper. It can parse the prototype executable and original ROM assets, but several gameplay routine meanings, controller/camera behavior, animation timing, effects, and collision details are still under active verification. The cat and floor meshes come from the original BCMDL files; the browser renderer is not Nintendo's original engine.
